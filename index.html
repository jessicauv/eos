Eos: Hear Our Changing Planet

I built a miniature physical museum that turns NASA images of our changing planet into music, designed for blind and low-vision visitors. Since you can't visit the physical one, I built this digital version so you can experience it too.

Best with headphones. Use Tab and Enter to navigate.
